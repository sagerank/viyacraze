OUR ORIGIN

Rejecting Fast Fashion. Building Permanent Form.

The contemporary streetwear landscape is flooded with disposable garments—thin 160 GSM cotton that distorts after three cycles in the wash, sagging collars, and plastic screen prints that peel away.

VIYACRAZE was founded with a singular conviction: every garment should hold its architectural shape like sculpture. We spent 14 months testing custom knit weaves, yarn twist counts, and neckline rib recoveries to establish our flagship 280 GSM French-Terry standard.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

THE FOUR PILLARS OF VIYACRAZE

Every piece is designed around four non-negotiable principles of luxury engineering.

01

Textile Rigor

Custom-milled 100% organic ring-spun combed cotton. The dense loop structure gives substantial drape and weight without trapping excess heat.

02

Architectural Cut

Extended drop-shoulder geometry calibrated to broaden the chest profile while contouring cleanly down the torso and waist.

03

Permanent Collar

2x2 spandex-infused rib knit collar reinforced with twin-needle herringbone neck tape to permanently eliminate sagging.

04

Archival Pigments

Multi-pass breathable water-based and metallic liquid chrome inks cured under high heat to become one with the cotton fiber.

ATELIER WORKFLOW

FROM RAW FIBER TO SIGNATURE DROP

A transparent journey through our small-batch artisanal manufacturing.

STEP 01

Long-Staple Yarn Selection

Only GOTS-certified organic cotton fibers exceeding 34mm in staple length are spun to prevent surface fuzzing and pilling.

STEP 02

Dense Loop Weaving

Knitted on specialized high-tension circular looms at 280 GSM for optimal fabric density and breathability.

STEP 03

3-Stage Enzyme Pre-Wash

Garments are thermally relaxed and pre-shrunk in natural bio-enzymes, guaranteeing exact true-to-size permanence.

STEP 04

Hand-Numbered Release

Each batch is strictly limited to 250 units worldwide. Each garment is hand-inspected and tagged with our golden seal.
