EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Clarifications regarding ordering via our official store, our 280 GSM fabric standard, sizing, and care.

All official purchases, inventory allocations, and encrypted payment processing are managed through our verified Online Storefront. Simply click "Official Store" or on any drop card to select your size and complete your order with UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, or Apple Pay/GPay.

Our T-shirts feature a tailored Architectural Drop-Shoulder Fit. They are slightly boxy through the chest and shoulders for a confident silhouette, with true-to-size length. If you prefer a standard tailored streetwear fit, order your normal size. For an exaggerated oversized skate/editorial drape, size up by one.

Standard retail t-shirts range from 140–180 GSM and easily lose their shape. Our 280 GSM heavyweight French terry provides substantial body, creates a clean drape without clinging, and retains crisp collar structure after countless washes.

Machine wash cold (30°C or below) inside out with gentle detergent. Hang dry in shade to preserve fabric density and print vibrancy. Do not iron directly over the graphic or 3D debossed insignia.

Each drop is strictly restricted to 250 units globally. Every garment arrives in a matte black archival presentation box complete with a hand-numbered Certificate of Authenticity and the golden VIYA insignia seal.
