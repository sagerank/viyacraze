YOUR DATA, OUR DUTY

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 30 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through our official store, contact us via the inquiry form, WhatsApp, or email, we collect only what is required to serve you: your name, contact details, delivery address, and order history. Payments are processed entirely by our store platform's payment gateway — we never see or store your full card or UPI credentials.

2. How We Use It

Your information is used to fulfil orders, provide sizing and client care over your preferred channel, share shipping updates, and — only with your consent — send you early access codes for new editions. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone.

3. Communications

By contacting us on WhatsApp or email you consent to replies on that channel. Every marketing message includes a way to opt out. You may request removal from our lists at any time by writing to concierge@viyacraze.com.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This website stores a small amount of data in your browser (service worker cache) purely to make the experience faster and available offline. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

5. Data Retention & Security

Order records are retained only as long as required for warranty, accounting, and legal purposes. Access to customer data is limited to the VIYACRAZE client care team.

6. Your Rights

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time via the email above, and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Accessibility Statement

We aim to keep this site perceivable, operable, and understandable for everyone: semantic markup, keyboard-navigable controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and text contrast tuned for readability. If anything on this site blocks you, tell us at concierge@viyacraze.com and we will fix it.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: concierge@viyacraze.com or via the contact page.
